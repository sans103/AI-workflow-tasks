Nav Redesign v3, feedback round applied

Nav v3: what changed and why

Responds to Slack feedback (Aug 5) and the naming call with Tosha (Aug 27). Core layout unchanged from the approved dark mega menu design.

1. Naming: Track Gear / Street Gear / Lifestyle

  • Per the call with Tosha: "Race" is out (alienates track day riders who don't race), and the product columns under both Custom Built and Ready To Wear are now Track Gear, Street Gear, and Lifestyle.
  • Track Gear: suits, gloves, boots, chest protectors, accessories (no jackets). Chest protectors and accessories stay under Track for now, may split later as the catalog grows.
  • Street Gear (new column): leather Jackets and street Gloves. Boots will join when street boots launch.
  • Lifestyle: sublimated jackets, tees, hoodies, hats and the rest of the casual range.
  • "Racing Gloves" is now "Gloves". Product titles keep their real names.
  • Dev note: street links point at the existing jacket and glove collections for now. Shopify needs street collections or tags (e.g. street gloves currently sit at the bottom of the main gloves collections) before these links can be scoped properly.

2. Product card images fixed

  • Featured cards now use a square image area with the full product visible (object-fit: contain on a light background). Nothing is cropped.

3. Badges trimmed, not removed

  • Removed: "High Value", "Hot", "Ships Now", and the "SALE" chip next to Pasture Sale.
  • Kept: "Best Seller" (on the Thor.2 card) and "Ships Fast" (on the Ready To Wear top pick), placed on products rather than column headers so they help users find the right item faster.
  • Column header icon chips removed. Columns are clean uppercase labels with a hairline underline, closer to the Tavi Active reference, while keeping Bison's dark theme.

4. Pasture confusion resolved

  • "The Pasture" and "Pasture Sale" links removed from Ready To Wear. RTW now focuses on in-line Bison gear, with a one-line pointer to The Pasture for deals.
  • The Pasture Sale menu now includes a plain-language explainer of Pasture Sale vs The Pasture.
  • "SALE" badge removed from the Pasture Sale nav item.

5. Maverick jacket journey fixed

  • Before: Ready To Wear's "Track Gear" column had no Jackets link at all, so a Maverick jacket was unreachable. Jackets is now in RTW > Gear (2 clicks: Ready To Wear, then Jackets).
  • The RTW featured card now links to the full Maverick Collection instead of a single suit.

Also folded in from the live site

  • Community updated to current pages: Internal Team and Education added; Trackside Schedule grouped under "Find Us" with Where To Find Us.
