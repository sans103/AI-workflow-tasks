Munchkin formulas are made using a unique nutrient‑blending approach, and while all infant formulas contain iron, the way minerals are combined can sometimes make harmless residue a bit more visible. What you’re seeing can happen when leftover formula sits out after a feeding. It’s the result of natural reactions in the bottle over time, not an issue with the formula itself. It can also happen from bottle to bottle depending on how the formula was prepared or how much the bottle was shaken during feeding. Importantly, this does not indicate spoilage, contamination, or a safety or quality concern with the formula itself.

A few simple tips can prevent this discoloration:

  • Use warm water (~100F) to prepare bottles—mixing with warm water helps the formula dissolve more easily
  • Only prepare what you need
  • Rinse bottles soon after use
  • Give the bottle a gentle swirl if feeding is paused to help reduce residue
  • Avoid leaving a used bottle out for more than about an hour
  • Toss leftover formula after each feeding
