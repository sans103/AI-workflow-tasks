Preparation & Feeding

Wash up first

Wash hands, and sterilize bottles and nipples before first use. Clean surfaces — tiny immune systems appreciate it.

Mix it right

Add 1 scoop per 2 fl oz of safe, lukewarm water. Level scoop, don't pack. Shake, don't stir — then test a drop on your wrist.

Storage rules (the ones that matter)

Use a prepared bottle within 1 hour of starting a feed. Mixed and refrigerated: use within 24 hours. Open can: use within 30 days.

Consistency is key

Babies love routine — same formula, same schedule, calmer feeds. Switching? Go gradual over 5–7 days.
