Safety at every step

From carefully sourced ingredients to the finished formula, every batch is tested against strict quality and safety standards. So you can feel confident with every bottle.

Our formula is produced to the standards required for infant nutrition in both the United States and Canada, and the facility is inspected under both regimes.

Each production batch is tested before release, and results are held on file so any can can be traced back to its run.

Every step of production happens under one roof in one of Canada's newest infant formula facilities, from blending through to sealing the can.

The formulation was evaluated in a 16 week clinical study. See the clinical sources for the peer reviewed research behind specific ingredients.
