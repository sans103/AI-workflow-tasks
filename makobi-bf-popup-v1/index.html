What changed and why

  • Offer: the 15% welcome offer becomes VIP early access to Black Friday (30% off sitewide on Thursday, Nov 26, one day before the public sale). Signups get a VIP tag so the client can send to them alone.
  • Look: black, red and Anton, built from the homepage Black Friday banner (same lineup, the red Cashay set in front, faded BLACK FRIDAY behind, red stars, white logo). Banner, bar and popup read as one sale.
  • One action per step: the control has two equal maroon buttons. Each new step has one red or white pill button, and the decline or skip is quiet underlined text. The baked VIP BLACK FRIDAY label is an outline pill so it never reads as a second button.
  • Brand fonts and shapes: the control uses Omnisend's Rubik and Fira Sans with maroon #A00619, which are not Makobi. The variant uses Anton for headlines, Roboto (the site's body font) for everything else, and the site's pill buttons and pill inputs.
  • Calmer later steps: step 2 and success drop the red number and shrink the headline, so the first screen does the selling and the next ones just finish the job.
  • SMS in its own step: email first, phone second. Omnisend requires that order, and a short first step converts better than asking for both at once.
  • Teaser: new. After a close, a red pill waits at the center of the left edge so people can come back to it.

What Omnisend's builder can do (checked Nov 12, 2026)

  • Layout: form width 300 to 1000px. One background image per form, placed left, right, top or full background, set to fill or fit. No custom CSS. Theme settings
  • Mobile: no separate mobile layout inside one form. Omnisend's advice is a second copy of the form set to Mobile only, with the original set to Desktop only. Mobile friendly popups
  • Steps: up to 4 steps. Email must come before SMS. Step 1 "Go to next step" saves the email right away; "Close form" keeps data from earlier steps. Success shows only after the last step is submitted. Multistep forms, Success messages
  • Items: Email, Phone Number (default country), Legal Consent (TCPA template, checkbox unchecked by default and must be ticked to submit), Text (Heading Large, Medium, Small, Paragraph, Footnote), Button (Submit form, Go to next step, Link to, Close form), Image (JPG, PNG or GIF up to 2000px), Line or Space. Form items
  • Styling: text blocks take font, size, line height and color (Create a popup). Primary, Secondary and Tertiary button styles with colors, border, custom corner radius, font, size, bold, padding and hover. Fields boxed or underlined with custom radius and their own colors. Close button can be shown or hidden and colored, always top right. Theme settings
  • Fonts: custom fonts can be uploaded in Brand Assets (WOFF, WOFF2, TTF, OTF, EOT) and used in forms. Anton and Roboto appear in Omnisend's font list. Brand assets, Fonts, August 2025 update
  • Teaser: text only, shape square (custom radius), circle or triangle, colors, border, font and size. Positions named in the doc: bottom left, bottom right, center left (given as examples, so the builder may offer more). Timing "Show after form is closed". Follows the form's device setting and stops once the visitor subscribes. Teaser
  • Rules: Time on Page, Scroll Depth, Exit Intent, Page Visits (any one fires the form), "Limit form appearance" frequency, scheduling with start and end date and time in the store's timezone, auto tag on submit, include or exclude segments. After someone subscribes through a form, a browser cookie stops it from showing again. Behavior, Display, Scheduling, Targeting

How this shaped the design: everything decorative (lineup, faded lettering, texture, stars, VIP pill, logo) is baked into uploaded images. Headlines, copy, fields, buttons and consent are native, so they stay editable, readable and accessible. Desktop and mobile are two forms.

Display rules to set

  • Schedule: start when the form is published; end Thursday, Nov 26 at 11:59 PM (store timezone). Then the form switches itself off.
  • Late signups: anyone who signs up on Nov 26 after the one time VIP send would never get the code. Recommended: an Omnisend automation triggered when a contact gets the tag "VIP Black Friday 2026" that sends the code to anyone tagged after the send goes out. The alternative is ending the form at the send time.
  • Old popup: switch it off at launch. Give it a scheduled start of Friday, Nov 27 at 12:00 AM and keep it enabled so it returns on its own. Omnisend's docs do not cover two forms overlapping, so the gap avoids it.
  • Trigger: Time on Page 6 seconds, or Scroll Depth 40%, or Exit Intent (whichever comes first).
  • Frequency: Limit form appearance, once every 2 days after a close. The teaser covers the time in between.
  • Stop after signup: Omnisend's targeting doc says a cookie stops a form from showing again in that browser once the visitor subscribes through it (Targeting). To also cover other devices, build a segment "tag is VIP Black Friday 2026" and exclude it in Visitor targeting; exclusion wins over inclusion.
  • Who: turn OFF "Don't show to existing contacts". Current subscribers are the best VIP prospects; they get tagged when they submit.
  • Tag: auto tag every submit "VIP Black Friday 2026". Omnisend tags contacts, it does not add them to a list, so the "dedicated list" is a segment on that tag.
  • Devices: desktop form Desktop only, mobile form Mobile only, with the same schedule, rules and tag on both.

Not possible natively, and what we did

  • Lineup, faded lettering, texture, stars, logo and the VIP pill: no layered art in the builder, so they are baked into images.
  • Letter spacing and font weights other than regular and bold are not offered, so native text uses default spacing and Roboto regular or bold only.
  • Line height: the popup doc lists it as adjustable. If the builder will not go down to 1.05, drop the step 1 headline to 34px on mobile and 38px on desktop so it stays two lines.
  • Red "30%" inside a white headline: the docs do not say if one word can take its own color. If not, set the whole headline white; red stays in the button and the art.
  • Close button size and position are fixed by Omnisend (top right). We can only color it, so the 44px tap target cannot be guaranteed. Test on a phone.
  • Teaser close button size is also fixed by Omnisend. The mock draws it at 20px; that is fine on desktop with a mouse, and on mobile it is a test item.
  • Checkbox style is Omnisend's default; the mock draws a plain white box.
  • The left image width on desktop is set by Omnisend. The mock assumes half the form (380px); the art is crop safe only if the image panel is 410px wide or less (wider crops the shoes, then the logo).
  • Teaser position: center left is the only documented spot that clears the sticky Add To Cart bar (bottom of every product page, on mobile and desktop) and Chat (bottom left). It floats over page content mid screen, which is the trade off.
  • Anyone who taps "Skip, email only" closes the form without seeing the success step. Their email is still saved and tagged.

Test before publishing

  • On a 375 x 667 phone, the step 1 GET EARLY ACCESS button sits above the fold (the board's last row renders 375 x 667 and 360 x 640).
  • Tapping the consent text toggles the checkbox, not only tapping the box itself.
  • Both error messages show, in #FF6B5E, right under the field or box.
  • "Nov 26" stays on one line in every step (Omnisend keeps the non breaking space).
  • Skip, email only: the contact is saved and tagged.
  • After a signup, the form and teaser stop showing in that browser, and on a second device once the segment exclusion is set.
  • Close buttons on the popup and the mobile teaser are easy to tap.

Open questions for the client

  1. SMS consent wording (legal): Omnisend's TCPA block always has a checkbox that must be ticked. The approved text starts "By clicking Text Me the Code". The mock shows it as "By checking this box and clicking Text Me the Code". Please confirm that change, the Terms and Privacy Policy URLs, and that a separate SMS terms page exists (Omnisend asks for one).
  2. Welcome 15%: keep or drop during the VIP campaign? It is not in any state of this design. If kept, which code, and note the MAKOBI15 cart script overwrites other codes.
  3. VIP tag name: is "VIP Black Friday 2026" fine, and will the Nov 26 email and SMS go to that segment only?
  4. Send time on Nov 26: who schedules the VIP email and SMS, and at what hour? The popup never states it.
  5. Late signups: set up the tag triggered automation so people who join after the send still get the code, or end the form at the send time?
  6. Sitewide: please confirm in Shopify that the VIP code has no product exclusions.
  7. After Nov 26: should the old 15% popup really return during the public Black Friday sale, or stay off until the weekend sale ends?
  8. Store timezone in Omnisend: confirm it matches the client's sale timezone, since the schedule uses it.
